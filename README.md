## 🌟 Overview

**Stock Trading Platform** is a modern financial technology application designed to simplify stock market analysis, portfolio monitoring, and trading-related decision-making.

The platform aims to provide an intuitive workspace for tracking market movements, analyzing stock performance, monitoring investments, and accessing actionable financial insights.

Built with a focus on scalability, performance, and user experience, this project brings essential stock market intelligence into one unified platform.

## ✨ Key Features

- 📊 **Market Dashboard** – Monitor important stock market indicators.
- 📈 **Stock Analysis** – Explore stock prices and performance trends.
- 💼 **Portfolio Management** – Track investment holdings and portfolio value.
- 🔍 **Stock Search** – Discover and explore individual stocks.
- 📉 **Market Trends** – Understand price movements and historical patterns.
- ⚡ **Real-Time Data Integration** – Support for live market data providers.
- 🧠 **Intelligent Insights** – Foundation for AI-powered market analysis.
